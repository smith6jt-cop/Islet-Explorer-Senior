2026-09-17. Track: defect 3 of three reported against the running app — *the app shows the wrong
UMAPs*. Every number below was produced by running something in this checkout; the commands are
named where they are not obvious.

## 3. The pooling decision

The question is not whether a 20-donor fit can be computed. It is whether the resulting axis is
composition or acquisition. This repository has been burned here before: the deployed single-cell
pseudotime turned out to be a batch axis at eta-squared **0.988**, and the pLN cluster
composition PC2 measures **0.975** on `acq_run`. Acquisition batch is aliased with disease status
at the coarse level — **all eight ND donors are in batch 1, and batch 2 contains no ND donor at
all** — so a coarse-batch number cannot be attributed on its own.

### How the number is computed

eta-squared on a 2-D embedding: between-group sum of squares over total, summed over both UMAP
axes. **The null is the load-bearing part.** Islets are nested in donors, so permuting islet
labels asks a question nobody has — it would call any donor-level grouping significant. The
**donor-to-group map** is permuted instead, group sizes preserved, 500 draws: the null says *how
much would an arbitrary partition of these same donors explain*. A grouping that is one donor per
group is then exactly its own null (p = 1) rather than a finding. This is implemented as
`embedding_eta2()` in `trajectory_islet.py` and is printed by every run.

### The candidate: one pooled 20-donor embedding

Fitted on the current typing, 8,665 islets above the 10-cell composition floor, 20 donors, same
recipe as the deployed fit (core ALR against the islet's own core endocrine count, Euclidean,
`n_neighbors = 30`).

| grouping | k | eta² | donor-block null mean | null p95 | p |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| coarse batch | 2 | **0.154** | 0.019 | 0.069 | 0.0020 |
| `stain_group` | 9 | 0.264 | 0.146 | 0.217 | 0.0040 |
| `acq_run` | 13 | 0.273 | 0.212 | 0.270 | 0.0499 |
| donor status | 3 | 0.257 | 0.040 | 0.105 | 0.0020 |
| donor | 20 | 0.305 | 0.305 | 0.305 | 1.0000 |

Coarse batch at 0.154 is aliased with status and **is not attributable as stated**. The only
place the two come apart is within a status that contains both batches, which here is T1D (four
batch-1 donors, five batch-2) and Aab+ (one and two):

| within | donors | donor eta² | coarse batch eta² | null mean | p |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| ND | 8 | **0.009** | not measurable — one batch | — | — |
| T1D | 9 | **0.276** | 0.063 | 0.030 | 0.0439 |
| Aab+ | 3 | 0.218 | 0.020 | 0.103 | 1.0000 |

### Is the T1D donor structure caused by pooling, or by the donors?

Measured rather than argued. Fitting **batch-1 donors only** — 5,099 islets, 13 donors, no batch-2
data present at all — and asking the same question:

```
within ND  (8 donors, 4,010 islets): donor eta2 = 0.016
within T1D (4 donors,   866 islets): donor eta2 = 0.334
```

The within-T1D donor structure is **higher** in a single-batch fit (0.334) than in the pooled one
(0.276). It is therefore not batch-2 contamination and not an artefact of pooling; it is that
these T1D donors differ from one another. No embedding of these donors will remove it.

### The alternative, also measured

The task names the alternative that worked in the pLN lane: cluster **within donor**, then relate
the clusters across donors. Done — Leiden 0.5 per donor on the same features, 89 within-donor
clusters over 19 donors (**6380 has 36 islets above the floor and cannot support a within-donor
embedding at all; it is Unavailable there**), then the cluster mean profiles agglomerated into 12
cross-donor classes.

Comparing the two constructions on the one attributable test — batch purity of a class, within
T1D, where the base rate is 0.74–0.75:

| construction | classes ≥30 T1D islets | >90% one coarse batch | median batch purity |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| pooled 20-donor, `leiden_0.5` | 9 | 2 | **0.84** |
| within-donor → cross-donor, K=12 | 9 | **8** | **1.00** |

**The within-donor alternative is worse here, and by a wide margin.** Relating donors through
their own cluster means makes each cross-donor class effectively a group of donors, and the donors
partition by batch — so the construction that was meant to remove batch reproduces it almost
perfectly. On the pooled fit, no cluster at any resolution is more than 80% one donor.

### The producer's own run agrees, and one bug was found in getting there

*(§5b: the fix below was made in the source but the artifact was not rebuilt, so the H5AD on disk
carried the broken null until a second pass reran it. The numbers here are the corrected ones and
have since been reproduced by a third, independent implementation.)*

The numbers above were measured with a standalone script. `trajectory_islet.py` now computes them
itself, and its run reproduces every one of them: coarse batch **0.154** (null 0.019, p 0.0020),
`stain_group` 0.264, `acq_run` 0.273, donor status 0.257, donor 0.305; within ND **0.009**, within
T1D **0.276** with coarse batch **0.063** (null 0.030, p 0.0439). Two independent implementations
of the same statistic, agreeing to three decimals — internal coherence, which is legitimate,
rather than a result reproduced.

Getting that agreement found a real bug in the first draft of `embedding_eta2()`: the permutation
was drawn *inside* the per-islet lookup, so every islet took its label from a different draw. That
turns the donor-block null straight back into the islet-level shuffle it exists to avoid. The
symptom is diagnostic and worth recording: every null mean collapsed to ~0.000 and every p-value
to 0.0020, **including the `donor` grouping, which is exactly its own null and must report
p = 1.0**. A grouping that cannot be significant coming back significant is how you know the null
is wrong. Fixed; the corrected run gives donor p = 1.0000.

### Decision

**Ship the pooled 20-donor embedding.** It is not a batch axis: the coarse figure of 0.154 is
aliased and unattributable, and where it *can* be attributed — within T1D — it is 0.063 against a
0.030 null. That is a detectable excess and it is reported, but it is not 0.988, and the
alternative measured at 1.00 median batch purity on the same test.

Three things ride with that decision and are not negotiable:

1. **It gates nothing.** The lane's own within-status donor ceiling is 0.15 with a three-donor
   minimum, and within T1D this embedding measures 0.276 on nine donors — a real variance
   decomposition, not the two-donor pairwise contrast the ten-donor build had to hedge. It fails
   that ceiling, and so does the batch-1-only fit at 0.334. The honest reading is that the ceiling
   is not achievable for T1D on these donors, not that a build passed.
2. **It is a composition map, not a disease ordering.** The Spatial card now says so in the UI.
3. **The coverage is stated in the UI**, counted off the frame being drawn, so a fit that covers
   fewer donors than the loaded cohort can never again draw silently.

### The pseudotime, unlike the clustering, does not survive the extension

Same run, same file. The ordering's own method checks:

```
all_islets_finite                  PASS
root_is_ND                         PASS
root_above_cell_floor              PASS
stable_under_neighbourhood_size    PASS      rho 0.978 against n_neighbors 15
stable_under_reroot                PASS      rho 0.954 median over the root pool, 0.841 min
pseudotime_is_not_mostly_donor     FAIL
```

The failure, in full:

| within | donors | donor eta² on the pseudotime |
|---|---:|---:|
| ND | 8 | 0.005 |
| Aab+ | 3 | **0.314** |
| T1D | 9 | **0.444** |

On the ten-donor cohort this check had a get-out: T1D held two donors, below the three-donor
minimum, so its 0.46 was reported and not gated — eta-squared over two donors is one pairwise
contrast, not a variance decomposition. **That get-out is gone.** All three statuses now clear
three donors, so all three are gated, and two of them are three times the 0.15 ceiling. The
producer therefore wrote `adata_ins_root.rejected.h5ad` and left the deployed H5AD untouched,
which is the behaviour it was built to have.

So the two halves of this fit part company, and they should be reported apart:

- **The islet-level clustering and its UMAP are rebuilt and shippable.** No cluster at any
  resolution is more than 80% one donor; the map is a description of composition and is captioned
  as one.
- **The 20-donor pseudotime ordering is not shippable** and is not being shipped. The Trajectory
  tab's two UMAPs therefore stay on the deployed ten-donor H5AD until an ordering exists that
  meets the check. That is a *stated* limitation now rather than a silent one — see §5 on the
  validity caption, which on this cohort had never rendered at all.

This is not a reason to lower the ceiling. The ceiling is the lane's own, it was met on ND
(0.005), and what fails it is nine T1D donors differing from one another — the same structure the
batch-1-only fit shows at 0.334. Widening the cohort made a previously unmeasurable quantity
measurable, and it came back over the line.

## 4. What extending the fit does to the ten donors already deployed

This is a real consequence and it is not small. Comparing the deployed
`islet_leiden.parquet` against each candidate, on the islets present in both:

| | refit, **same ten donors**, current typing | pooled, **all twenty** |
|---|---:|---:|
| islets in both | 4,354 | 4,354 |
| deployed islets that lose a position | 17 | 17 |
| islets that gain one | 262 | 212 |
| `leiden_0.3` ARI | 0.441 | 0.404 |
| `leiden_0.5` ARI | 0.385 | 0.386 |
| `leiden_0.8` ARI | 0.401 | 0.335 |
| `leiden_1.0` ARI | 0.315 | 0.288 |
| mean share of an islet's 30 embedding neighbours preserved | **0.178** | **0.147** |

Read the first column carefully: **most of the change is the typing update, not the pooling.**
Refitting the same ten donors with nothing but the current cell table already moves the
`leiden_0.5` partition to ARI 0.385 and preserves only 17.8% of each islet's embedding
neighbourhood. Holding typing fixed and extending the fit from ten donors to twenty costs
comparatively little: refit-10 against pooled-20 on their 4,616 shared islets gives ARI **0.624 /
0.723 / 0.634 / 0.644** across the four resolutions.

(An earlier draft of this table read 4,311 in the pooled column. That number is real but it is
scoped to a different frame — it is the positions the 20-donor rebuild holds that the deployed file
never had *anywhere*, not the gains among the 7,287 deployed islets the rebuild also carries. Under
a header that says "islets present in both" it was the wrong count, and it is corrected to 212.)

**So: any cluster label a reader has written down from the deployed map is void.** Not shifted —
void. A cluster "5" in the new file is not a renumbering of cluster "5" in the old one, and no
mapping between them is published because none is faithful at ARI 0.39.

One more consequence of the typing update, in the same direction: the deployed fit's own donor
eta² is 0.112, and the same ten donors refitted on the current typing measure **0.027**. The
deployed map carried donor structure that the current cell table does not support.

## 5. What changed, and where it has to land

**Producer** — `core/phenocycler/scripts/typing/trajectory_islet.py`:

- `--islets` (which proportions to fit on), `--out-dir` and `--leiden-name`, so a rebuild can be
  fitted and inspected without overwriting a deployed file while a question about it is open.
- `embedding_eta2()` and a printed block: the pooled table above, plus the within-status pass, on
  every run. The pseudotime already carried a donor eta²; the UMAP is a different object and the
  app draws it, so it now gets its own number. Also recorded in `uns['embedding_batch']`.
- `BATCH2_DONORS` derives from `cohort.BATCH2_REEXPORTED` rather than restating a donor list.

Run as:

```bash
.venv/bin/python core/phenocycler/scripts/typing/trajectory_islet.py --include-marginal \
  --islets  data/development/typing/umap_rebuild_2026-09-17/islet_celltype_proportions_20donor.csv \
  --out-dir data/development/typing/umap_rebuild_2026-09-17 \
  --leiden-name islet_leiden_20donor.parquet
```

**Rebuilt artifact** — `data/development/typing/umap_rebuild_2026-09-17/`:

| file | what |
|---|---|
| `islet_leiden_20donor.parquet` | the rebuilt islet-level clustering + UMAP, 20 donors: 17,786 islets, 8,665 with a position |

**App** — `mod_spatial_server.R`:

- A resolver that prefers `islet_leiden_20donor.parquet` **in `domain_data_root()`** and

## 5b. Re-run of this track: what a second pass found in its own work

The producer was corrected *after* the artifact was built, and the artifact was not rebuilt. So
`adata_ins_root.rejected.h5ad` (17:24) still carried the **buggy** donor-block null in
`uns['embedding_batch']` — `coarse_batch` null mean 0.00011, `donor` p = 0.0020 — the exact
signature §3 describes as how you know the null is wrong, sitting inside the file kept "so the
failure can be inspected". A write-up describing a fix is not the fix landing in the artifact.

Rerun with the corrected code. `uns['embedding_batch']` now records what the text says:

```
coarse_batch   k=2   eta2=0.154   null mean=0.019 p95=0.069  p=0.0020
stain_group    k=9   eta2=0.264   null mean=0.144 p95=0.222  p=0.0040
acq_run        k=13  eta2=0.273   null mean=0.211 p95=0.270  p=0.0459
donor_status   k=3   eta2=0.257   null mean=0.041 p95=0.108  p=0.0020
donor          k=20  eta2=0.305   null mean=0.305 p95=0.305  p=1.0000
within Aab+ (3 donors): donor 0.218; coarse_batch 0.020 (null 0.103, p=1.0000)
within ND   (8 donors): donor 0.009; coarse batch Unavailable here -- one batch only
within T1D  (9 donors): donor 0.276; coarse_batch 0.063 (null 0.030, p=0.0439)
```

`donor` p = 1.0000 is the thing to look at: a grouping that is exactly its own null reporting
p = 1 is what the broken version could not do.

**A third, independent implementation agrees.** Written from the parquet alone — not sharing a
line with `embedding_eta2()` — it gives coarse batch 0.154 (null 0.019, p 0.0020), `stain_group`
0.264 (null 0.144), `acq_run` 0.273 (null 0.211, p 0.0459), donor status 0.257, donor 0.305
(p 1.0000); within ND 0.009, within Aab+ 0.218 with coarse batch 0.020 (p 1.0000), within T1D
0.276 with coarse batch 0.063 (null 0.030, p 0.0439). Three implementations, agreeing to three
decimals, with the permutation draw the only source of disagreement. Internal coherence, which
is legitimate — none of this is a biological result being reproduced. **The pooling decision of
§3 stands unchanged on a measurement that has now been made three times.**

### The rebuild is reproducible, and the shipped file did not move

Two runs of the producer on the same input, compared column by column on all 17,786 rows:

```
case_id islet_key leiden_0.3 leiden_0.5 leiden_0.8 leiden_1.0 leiden_umap_1 leiden_umap_2
  1.000000   1.000000   1.000000   1.000000   1.000000   1.000000      1.000000      1.000000
```

Identical, nulls included; `leiden_umap_*` max absolute difference **0**. So the correction
changed the H5AD's recorded statistic and nothing about what the app would draw. Worth stating
because the naive check said otherwise: comparing the label columns with `astype(str)` reported
48.7% agreement, which is exactly 8,665/17,786 — the *positioned* fraction. Under the `str` dtype
the two files' nulls do not compare equal to each other, so the disagreement was entirely the
9,121 unpositioned islets comparing null against null. A null-aware comparison, and an ARI of
1.0000 at every resolution with identical cluster sizes per id, is what settles it. A
reproducibility alarm that is really a dtype artefact is worth one line so nobody raises it twice.

This is the leak `apps/islet/CLAUDE.md` names — a cohort's size, its donor ids and its method
printed as a measurement of whichever dataset was loaded — and it is worth noting that it is not
only an app-side hazard: the producer that feeds the app can hold it just as well, where no
testthat assertion will ever see it. The `cohort_limit` sentence also quietly carried the old
build's *exemption* ("with only two T1D donors that is one pairwise contrast"), which §3 shows is
precisely what stopped being true — so the file would have told a reader the failing gate was
excused.

All three are derived per run now:

- `cohort_limit` from `within_status_donor_count`, `within_status_donor_eta2`, the gated list and
  the two constants. On this build: *"9 T1D, 8 ND, 3 Aab+ donors (20 in the fit) … within ND
  (8 donors) donor explains 0.5% of the pseudotime — gated; within T1D (9 donors) … 44.4% —
  gated … the ceiling on a gated status is 0.15."*
- `batch_correction_reason` from the actual test the old sentence asserted: which staining groups
  hold **a single donor within a status**, run on the cohort in front of the script. On this build
  that names nine such groups across the three statuses and reports S0, S4, S6, S7 as the groups
  that do carry within-group disease contrast.
- `composition_floor_sweep` counts this fit's own islets at each candidate floor — 17,786 / 12,213
  / **8,665** / 6,099 / 4,812 / 3,423 at 0, 5, 10, 20, 30, 50 — and records `rho` **only at the
  floor actually used** (0.978), `None` elsewhere, rather than carrying another cohort's numbers.
  A missing measurement is unavailable, not a borrowed one.

The ten-donor sweep table stays in the module docstring, where it is the *evidence for the chosen
value* rather than a claim about the current run.

Nothing reads these three keys today — the app's validity caption renders gates, eta-squared, rho
and the MWU p, not these strings — so no figure was captioned with them. They were wrong in the
file, which is enough.
