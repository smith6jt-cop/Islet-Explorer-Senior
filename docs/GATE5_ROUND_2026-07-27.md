> ## Review-figure defects found by the maintainer, 2026-07-28 — three fixed, all now guarded
>
> Every one of these was in the *display* path, found only by opening the finished PDFs. None touched a
> divisor, a call, or a compartment. All three are now asserted in `tests/test_restore_validation.py`
> (247 tests), and `scripts/gate5/preview_review_row.py` renders ONE row through the production path in
> ~1 minute so a display change can be judged before a ~50-minute rebuild.
>
> 1. **The Step-2 line was drawn at the manuscript MAXIMUM, not the divisor production uses.**
>    `restore_pair_review.py` drew `target_full_stats["maximum"]`, and `restore_validation.py` stretched
>    the y-axis to `1.05 ×` that same maximum. These coincided while the policy was `max`; frozen at
>    `p99` (2026-07-27) they diverged by a **median 6×, up to 200×**. Consequences: the line sat at
>    y≈0.95 on *every* panel by construction, showing a threshold production does not use; and the data
>    cloud was crushed into the bottom of the axis on **283 of 479** VALID rows (153 below 10%).
>    Worst case 6591 `CD11b ← Pan_Cytokeratin`: max 54,104 against a p99 divisor of 1,532, so an axis
>    56,809 tall put the entire cloud below y=0.04 — it read as "no CD11b on the plot". The same donor
>    and marker looked fine against CD3e only because that control set had no comparable outlier
>    (max 2,411). Both now use the active divisor: that panel's axis is [432, 2899] with Step 2 at
>    0.446 of its height.
>
> 2. **Marker crops were windowed so background rendered as signal.** The black point was the 1st
>    percentile of positive pixels — *below* the background, so ~99% of pixels rendered non-black — and
>    gamma 0.65 then lifted the dim end further. A weak channel became a bright wash, defeating the one
>    purpose of the panel. The window is now anchored to the workflow's own measurement: **black point =
>    that donor-marker's raw input floor** (`linear_otsu`, or `max(Otsu, Triangle)` per marker policy —
>    the same floor the method uses to split the arms), upper bound = the 99.8th percentile of pixels
>    *above* that floor, and **linear (gamma 1.0)**. A crop with nothing above the floor now renders
>    black rather than having its noise amplified into apparent signal. `_display_floors` **raises** on a
>    missing floor rather than silently reverting to the percentile window.
>
> 3. **The figure legend overflowed both page edges.** Labels run to ~150 characters and the legend was
>    drawn `loc="center"`, which matplotlib does not shrink to fit. Now hard-wrapped with a measured
>    fit check. A standalone `FIGURE_LEGEND.png` was written beside the old bundle so it stayed readable
>    in the meantime.
>
> **Not a defect, but a real source of confusion:** "double-high" is defined by the **raw input floors**,
> while the visible quadrants are drawn by **Step 1** (vertical) and **Step 2** (horizontal). Those are
> different boundaries, so double-high cells do not occupy the top-right quadrant by construction — on
> the fixed 6591 Pan-CK panel they are the pink cluster beginning at y≈0.17, which is exactly where
> CD11b's floor of 855 falls on the new axis.

## Finding 1 — the islet-core Immune gradient is aliased with acquisition batch

**This is the most consequential result of the round, and it bears directly on the study's question.**

Islet-core Immune separates by disease exactly as hoped: ND 3.7–7.1% (all 8 donors), Aab+ 8.5–19.6%,
T1D 4.1–21.0%. Kruskal **p = 0.0094**.

It also separates by acquisition software, and *more strongly*: Fusion 2.3.1 median 7.0, Fusion 2.4.0
median 4.3, Fusion 2.4.1 median 19.3. Kruskal **p = 0.0016**.

The two cannot be told apart in this cohort, and the stratified view (figure `d1`, right panel) shows why:

| software | ND | non-ND |
|---|---|---|
| Fusion 2.3.1 | n=2, 3.9–4.7% | n=3, 7.0–11.9% |
| Fusion 2.4.0 | n=6, 3.7–7.1% | **n=2, 4.1–5.2%** |
| Fusion 2.4.1 | **n=0** | n=7, 8.5–21.0% |

Within Fusion 2.4.0 the two T1D donors (6593 4.1%, 6436 5.2%) sit **inside** the range of its six ND
donors. Fusion 2.4.1 holds every high-immune donor and contains no ND donor at all. So the cohort-level
disease signal is carried almost entirely by a between-software difference, and the one stratum with
donors on both sides of the disease line shows no separation.

**This does not say the biology is absent.** It says this cohort's design cannot separate the two, and
that no disease claim about peri-islet or islet-core immune infiltration is safe until it is analysed
batch-aware. `donor_acquisition_batch.csv` already records the covariate; nothing was using it.

## Finding 2 — CD11b is under-called about four-fold, and the mechanism is identifiable

CD11b sits at 2.1% (0.8–3.7%) against the **7–10% the Gate-3 full-cell pilot validated** for
`CD11b ← EpCAM`. Comparing the pilot to production on the same 7 donors and the same pairs (figure `d2`):

- Every marker's divisor **fell** when the policy moved to `reference_only`/p99 — CD3e 0.23–0.88×,
  CD31 0.18–0.68×, B3TUBB 0.27–0.72×, E-cadherin 0.54–0.83×, CD68 0.48–1.12×.
- **CD11b's rose, 1.46–2.25×.** It is the only marker that moved that way.

p99 of a set cannot exceed its max, so a rising divisor means the *control population* changed, not the
statistic. It did: `control_definition` went `reference_and_target` → `reference_only`, removing the cap
at the target's own Otsu floor. For a marker with high channel background — which the maintainer has
recorded for CD11b on 6591 — that admits its own background into its own negative control.

Two corroborations: CD11b's divisor spans **30.6×** across the 20 donors (799 → 24,411), the widest of
any gate marker, which is what a divisor tracking background rather than signal looks like; and its
`target_fold` (2.89) and `reference_fold` (2.5) are the weakest in the accepted set.

This matters because CD11b is **load-bearing**: 83% of the cells it calls are called by no other Immune
gate marker (figure `d4/d5`). Restoring it toward the validated level would move the Immune compartment.

## Finding 3 — SST's weakness is a per-donor pass/fail, not the disease effect the handoff describes

The handoff records: "target_fold median 1.68 in ND … against 20.7 in T1D and 55.2 in AAB. Delta cells
should not be weakest in non-diabetic pancreas."

Those medians are real but they summarise a **bimodal** quantity. Sorted across all 20 donors, SST
`target_fold` is 0.94, 1.12, 1.17, 1.40, 1.43, 1.46, 1.55, 1.81 — then jumps to 2.54, 7.82, 9.74, 12.56,
12.73, 20.65, 34.51, 46.46, 55.20, 66.99, 108.34, 117.20. **Nothing lies between 1.81 and 2.54.** SST
either separates its arms or it does not.

The failures are not disease-specific: ND 5/8, Aab+ 1/3, T1D 2/9 — **Fisher p = 0.167**. Comparing group
medians of a split distribution manufactured the disease pattern.

SST is also nearly inert: it uniquely claims 3.0% of its own islet-core positives, 1.0% of islet-core
cells. Whatever is decided, it cannot move the Epithelial compartment much.

## Finding 4 — CD34 is the whole of the endothelial rise, and most of it is not vessel

**Maintainer, from the images: all CD31 vessel-like structure is CD34⁺, but much CD34 is CD31⁻.** Both
halves measured over all 25.1 M cells, and both hold:

| | |
|---|---|
| of CD31⁺ cells, also CD34⁺ | **85.8%** |
| of CD34⁺ cells, also CD31⁺ | **39.7%** |
| CD34⁺CD31⁻ cells | **2,643,946 — 10.5% of every cell in the cohort** |
| …of those, Vimentin⁺ | 44.5% |
| …of those, in islet core | **1.0%** |

CD34 agrees with CD31 wherever CD31 fires, and then adds a population two and a half times larger that
CD31 never sees, is nearly half Vimentin⁺, and **avoids islets — where vessels are densest**. That is
the signature of a fibroblast/stromal population, not endothelium.

Because Endothelial gates second, those cells are taken from Epithelial and Mesenchymal. Removing CD34
from the gate (counterfactual over all 20 donors, no other change):

| arm | Immune | Endothelial | Epithelial | Neural | Mesenchymal | `Other` | islet-core Endothelial |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| production | 21.3 | **13.5** | 49.4 | 0.5 | **2.0** | 13.3 | 10.8 |
| CD34 out of Endothelial | 21.3 | **7.4** | 51.2 | 0.8 | **3.9** | 15.4 | 8.6 |
| CD11b out of Immune | 19.7 | 13.7 | 50.6 | 0.5 | 2.0 | 13.5 | 10.8 |
| both out | **19.7** | **7.5** | 52.5 | 0.8 | **4.0** | 15.5 | 8.6 |

Endothelial returns to the 2–6% range CD31 alone gave, Mesenchymal nearly doubles, and the positive
biological control **survives** — endothelium stays enriched inside islets (8.6% core vs 7.4% overall).
That control was the strongest evidence the endothelial call was sound, and it does not depend on CD34.

## Finding 4b — the original CD34 distribution evidence

The handoff flags tissue Endothelial 13.3% (from 2–6% under CD31 alone) and names CD34 as the likely
cause. Quantified:

- CD34 uniquely claims **9.1% of all cells** — cells no other Endothelial gate marker calls. CD31
  uniquely claims 0.9%, Podoplanin 1.2%. CD34's unique share of its own positives is **56%**.
- So CD34 alone accounts for essentially the entire increase.
- Its distribution has **one** local maximum per donor; CD31's has **two** on every donor. Read the
  `divisor_context.pdf` pages side by side: CD31 has a resolved positive shoulder, CD34 is a broad
  graded continuum with the divisor cutting mid-slope.

A marker without a resolved positive population is exactly the case where the divisor choice decides the
answer. This is not proof CD34 is over-calling — fibroblast CD34 expression is real and so is
endothelial — but it is the marker where the threshold matters most and it has had no image review.

## Finding 5 — EpCAM's weakness is now low-risk, for a reason worth recording

EpCAM has the weakest arm separation of the epithelial markers (`target_fold` 3.15 against 20–27 for the
keratins) and `target_low_sbr` on 3 donors. The anatomy check promoted it on the argument that it
recovers endocrine epithelium the keratins miss. Both halves check out:

- EpCAM is called on **43.3% of islet-core cells** against 25.4% cohort-wide — enriched in islets.
- Ker8_18 and Pan-Cytokeratin collapse from 47.6% / 35.3% cohort-wide to **7.2% / 7.1%** in islet core.

But the same expansion added the hormones, and they now do that job directly: in islet core INS uniquely
claims 6.2% of cells and GCG 5.6%, while **EpCAM uniquely claims 0.2%**. The argument that promoted
EpCAM was made against a 4-marker union that did not yet contain INS/GCG/SST. Under the gate that
actually shipped, EpCAM is nearly redundant in the region it was promoted for — so its weak separation
is low-risk, and the anatomy check's reasoning should not be re-quoted as if it still applied.

## Finding 6 — the islet-core spread is one question, not two

The anatomy check noted the wide per-donor spread in islet-core Epithelial as an open worry. In the
shipped gate set, islet core is essentially a three-way partition: Immune + Epithelial + Endothelial =
**97.1–99.9%** of core cells on every donor, and r(Immune, Epithelial) = **−0.969**.

The four donors with the lowest islet-core Epithelial (6534 60.5%, 6591 64.1%, 6566 66.3%, 6450 67.5%)
are exactly the four with the highest islet-core Immune (20.7, 19.3, 21.0, 19.6%). The Epithelial
"deficit" is the Immune gain, cell for cell. So "is 60% Epithelial too low?" and "are those Immune calls
real?" are the same question — and by Finding 1 it is also the question the batch confound sits on.

## Finding 7 — INS is unsupported on four T1D donors, and they are treated three different ways

The handoff carries 6566 INS as the cohort's single `NO_TARGET_EXPRESSION_CONFIRMED`, provisionally,
pending image review. The dashboard shows the disposition is less principled than it looks.

| donor | `target_fold` | `divisor_reproducibility` | state | called |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6380 T1D | **0.92** | 0.998 | VALID | 0.12% |
| 6623 T1D | 1.08 | 0.985 | VALID | 2.77% |
| 6534 T1D | 1.10 | 0.983 | VALID | 2.83% |
| 6566 T1D | **1.67** | **0.690** | `NO_TARGET_EXPRESSION_CONFIRMED` | 0% |
| every other donor | 8.4–296 | ≥ 0.999 | VALID | 0.4–6.3% |

INS's arm separation collapses on exactly four donors, all T1D — consistent with beta-cell loss, and
`divisor_context.pdf` page 19 shows it directly: every ND and Aab+ donor has a distinct beta-cell mode
near 10³, and on 6566 that mode is **absent**, with 6380 and 6623 nearly so.

But **6566 is not the worst of the four — 6380 is**, at fold 0.92. 6566 reached a non-VALID state
through `divisor_reproducibility = 0.690` (a *technical* instability), and the expression review then
converted that into a biological absence. The other three never left `VALID`, because METHOD v9
deliberately demoted a low arm fold from an abstention to a reported flag. So the single biological
absence in the entire 480-pair run was selected by a technical gate, and the donor with the weakest INS
evidence still emits calls.

The three outcomes on near-identical evidence — 0%, 0.12%, 2.8% — are not defensible as biology. This
needs one image review covering all four donors, not just 6566, and one rule applied to all of them.

## Finding 8 — the accounting is exact, and 30% of cells are decided by the ordering alone

`scripts/gate5/audit_lineage.py` (new) asserts Gate 6's exit criterion rather than assuming it.
**It passes:** all **25,151,028** canonical cells are assigned exactly once, no duplicate `object_id`, no
canonical cell missing, nothing assigned that is not canonical, `assignment_reason` partitions every
cell, and on every donor `Other` is *exactly* the population with no positive gate marker (zero
`Unresolved`, so the two must coincide, and they do). The QuPath export independently reproduces the
same 25,151,028 across 20 images.

What it also shows is larger than the accounting. **7,535,359 cells — 30.0% of the cohort — had two or
more compartments eligible**, and which one they landed in was decided by `COMPARTMENT_ORDER`, not by
evidence discriminating between them. Per compartment, over all 25.1M cells:

| compartment | a gate marker is positive | actually assigned | lost to a higher gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Immune (1st) | 21.5% | 21.5% | — |
| Endothelial (2nd) | 20.3% | 13.2% | **−7.1 pp** |
| Epithelial (3rd) | 61.8% | 49.3% | **−12.5 pp** |
| Neural (4th) | 2.2% | 0.3% | **−1.9 pp (87% of its eligible cells)** |
| Mesenchymal (5th) | 20.5% | 2.0% | **−18.5 pp (90% of its eligible cells)** |

**Mesenchymal is 2% of cells only because it is last.** One in five cells is Vimentin⁺ or SMA⁺, and nine
in ten of those are claimed by Immune, Endothelial or Epithelial first. Some of that is correct biology —
Vimentin is expressed on endothelium and leukocytes, which is why it sits last — but at this magnitude
"Mesenchymal = 2%" is a statement about the ordering, not a measurement of fibroblast abundance, and it
should not be reported as one. The same holds for Neural at 0.3%.

This is the same mechanism the anatomy check found when it moved Endothelial ahead of Epithelial, and it
is not confined to that one pair.

### How to fix it — three levers, measured, largest first

Where the 5.15 M Vimentin⁺/SMA⁺ cells actually end up:

| claimed by | share of Vimentin⁺/SMA⁺ cells |
|---|---|
| Immune | **44.8%** |
| Endothelial | 36.3% — of which **11.1 pp via CD34 with no CD31 and no Podoplanin** |
| Mesenchymal (keeps them) | 9.7% |
| Epithelial | 8.1% |
| Neural | 1.1% |

1. **Drop CD34 from the Endothelial gate.** The one change with a large, clean effect: Mesenchymal
   2.0% → 3.9%, and it is justified on its own terms by Finding 4 rather than to make Mesenchymal
   bigger. This is the actionable lever.
2. **Accept that Vimentin cannot be fixed by reordering.** 44.8% of the loss is to Immune, and Vimentin
   is genuinely expressed on leukocytes — that is *why* Mesenchymal sits last. Moving it earlier would
   corrupt Immune, which carries the study. Even SMA-only cells (4.06% of all cells, the more specific
   mural marker) lose **80.8%** to higher gates, so this is not Vimentin's promiscuity alone.
3. **Stop resolving it silently.** `gate6_tie_breaks.csv` now records, per donor and compartment, how
   many cells were eligible versus claimed. The cheapest honest fix is to carry a multi-eligible flag
   per cell so any downstream analysis can exclude or report those 30%, instead of the ordering
   deciding invisibly.

The structural point stands: with markers this non-exclusive, an ordered residual cannot do better than
its order. If Mesenchymal and Neural are to be *reported* numbers rather than residual leftovers, they
need markers the earlier compartments do not share — and this panel does not obviously have them.

## What this settles, and what it does not

**Settled.** Gate 5 has a reviewable surface, per donor, with every flag donor-local. CD11b's shortfall
has a mechanism and a reproduction. SST's disease framing is withdrawn. CD34 is quantified as the whole
endothelial rise. EpCAM's promotion rationale no longer describes the shipped gate. INS's four
weak donors are identified, and the asymmetry in how they were dispositioned is on the record.
Gate 6's cell accounting is proven exact, and the screen behind the review bundle is proven to
reproduce the production apply bit-for-bit on all 480 accepted donor-pairs.

**Not settled.** Whether CD11b should be restored — that is a divisor-policy question, and the policy is
frozen cohort-wide, so a CD11b-specific fix means either a per-marker control definition (which the
method does not have) or reopening the policy. **Do not change it on this evidence alone.**

**Unmeasured, still.** Accuracy. Everything above is internal consistency, distribution shape, and
cohort design. None of it says what fraction of cells called Immune are immune cells — which Finding 1
now makes urgent rather than merely outstanding. `data/gold_standard_v1/` is built and blinded and needs
annotator time.

### The scorer was broken, and would have failed only after annotation

`scripts/gold_standard/score.py` was dry-run end to end on synthetic annotations (two fabricated
annotators over the real `sample_key.csv`, scored, then discarded). Inter-annotator reporting works —
κ = 0.846 on the synthetic pair, unsure and disagreement counted and excluded as the protocol promises.

The pipeline half did not. It defaulted to `--arm-root data/_arm --arm ctrl-reference_only_div-p99
--design union`: an **anatomy-check arm**, built with the old 7-marker gate set, and the anatomy check's
4-marker epithelial comparator. It crashed on the 14 marker `_state` columns that shipped since — and a
crash is the lucky outcome, because the alternative was quietly scoring a configuration that no longer
exists. Fixed to default to the **production** gated output and `config.COMPARTMENT_GATES` as it ships;
the arm and the 4-marker union are retained as explicit opt-in overrides for reproducing that round.

This is worth noting as a pattern: the instrument for measuring accuracy had itself gone stale against
the thing it measures, and only running it revealed that. It is now proven to run before any annotator
time is spent on it.

   - **CD11b out of the Immune gate** (retraction above). 81% of its positives are epithelial-marker⁺
     and 79% carry no other immune marker; Immune falls 21.3% → 19.7%.
