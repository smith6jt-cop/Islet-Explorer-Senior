### Ingest: not measured, and blocked as things stand

No log records an ingest wall clock. `core/phenocycler/data/` contains no `.log` file, the run
framework's `run.json` carries no durations, and `~/.bash_history` has no timestamps
(`python -m phenocycler.pipeline run --config config.ini --pipelined` at line 1703, undated). The
I/O floor is small — a full linear read of the 147.9 GB `Cellmeasurements.csv` took **63 s**
(13:51:30 → 13:52:33, ≈ 2.35 GB/s), and `wc -l` over the 11.83 GB `6579.tsv` took **4.85 s** — but
DuckDB parsing ~994–1228 columns is the real cost and I did not measure it. **Treat ingest as
unknown; do not quote a number for it.**

The operational obstacle is not time, it is a guard:

```
core/phenocycler/phenocycler/cells_parquet.py:438-441
    if cfg.cells_dir.exists() and any(cfg.cells_dir.iterdir()):
        raise FileExistsError(
            f"{cfg.cells_dir} is not empty; ingest is immutable and will not merge with a prior run")
```

`--force` does not help: `run_stage` (`core/phenocycler/phenocycler/pipeline.py:535-552`) prints
`[force]` and then calls `build_cells_parquet(context.config)`, which raises anyway. So **ingest
cannot add a donor to a populated `cells` root.** `core/phenocycler/data/cells` currently holds
`donor_id=0112` and `donor_id=6533`.

Two routes, both a maintainer decision:

1. **Scratch `data_dir`, then move the partition.** Point a 6579-only manifest and a config with
   `data_dir = <scratch>` at the revised TSV, ingest 6579 alone, move
   `<scratch>/cells/donor_id=6579` into `core/phenocycler/data/cells/`. This is the metabolic-manifest
   pattern and costs one donor's ingest.
2. **Empty and re-ingest the whole root.** Scans every contracted CSV — 147.9 + 64.2 + 3.4 GB plus
   the three TSVs, ≈ 240 GB — and re-derives 0112 and 6533 as well. Strictly more expensive and it
   throws away two finished partitions.

Stages 2–4 have no such guard: `run_stage` skips a stage whose *root* is non-empty, so they need
`--force`, but `context.donors` comes from the manifest, so a 6579-only manifest under `--force`
recomputes 6579 only. `write_donor_partition` leaves 0112 and 6533 untouched.

`controls`, `calibrate`, `type` and export stay blocked by `NO_VERIFIED_TYPING`; nothing here moves
that latch. Bringing 6579 into `data/development/typing/cells/` afterwards is a separate,
unscoped job.

## Recommendation

**Do not restore 6579 from the archive. Re-run it — but from `data/RevisedQuPathExports/6579.tsv`,
not from the qptiff.**

The archived `cells` partition is genuine, complete and correctly counted at 2,041,786 rows, and it
is nonetheless the wrong data: it carries 1,151 islets and 51,154 islet-resident cells against the
7,949 islets and 173,175 islet-resident cells in today's export, a 3.39× difference in the
population this study is about. It is also in the retired 82-column schema. Reusing it would put a
June answer next to September answers with nothing marking the difference — the failure the
2026-09-15 retirement exists to stop.

The qptiff does not need to be touched. No stage reads it for pixels: ingest reads the measurement
export, geometry reads pixel sizes from the manifest, REDSEA reads the per-cell geojson — and
6579's is on disk at 3.86 GB with 99.80% object-id agreement with the revised export, so the June
segmentation is still the right one. A 113 GB re-export from the image would be re-doing work that
completed at 13:33 today.

Concretely, and all of it a maintainer call:

1. Add 6579 to `core/phenocycler/cohort-spec.json` `expected_donors` **before** anything regenerates
   `apps/islet/config/cohort.json`, or the donor silently disappears from the app roster (§6a).
2. Build a 6579-only manifest against `data/RevisedQuPathExports/6579.tsv`, `panel-cd38-59ch`, the
