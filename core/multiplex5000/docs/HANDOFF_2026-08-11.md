## The one thing to understand first

**Otsu was already dropped once, and reinstating RESTORE brought it back by
accident.**

- `ac00c18` "Replace RESTORE with donor-local cell typing workflow" deleted
  `raw_otsu_floor`, `MARKER_INPUT_FLOOR_METHODS` and all of
  `restore_validation.py`, and introduced `marker_calibration.py` instead.
- `ec97cbc` (2026-08-10) reinstated `restore_validation.py` **wholesale**, which
  restored the Otsu floors and sidelined the successor.

The successor is `marker_calibration.empirical_upper_threshold` — a finite-sample
upper-tail **order statistic** on the pair's own control at a per-marker alpha,
which *refuses* (returns `None` from `_empirical_order_index`) when the control
cannot resolve that alpha. With `empirical_tail_probability`, a bootstrap
interval, and four explicit fail states. **Do not delete `marker_calibration.py`**
— an earlier plan of mine proposed exactly that.

Scored on synthetic data where the positive count is known:

| positives | Otsu floor | order-statistic successor |
|---|---|---|
| **0.3%** (CD3e, MPO, CD8 regime) | **88.66× truth** | **1.36×** |
| 5% (CD31) | 1.00× | 1.02× |
| 30% (E-cadherin) | 1.00× | 1.00× |

Otsu is correct wherever positives are common — that is why it survived review.
At 0.3% the p99.5 clip in `raw_otsu_floor` puts the whole positive mode inside
the terminal histogram bin, so the argmax lands in the background. Sixteen
markers are on pure Otsu, including CD31, E-cadherin and Vimentin.

**Alpha needs no invention.** `MarkerRegistry.marker_alpha` already allocates a
0.01 familywise budget across each gate's members — CD3e 0.001, CD31 0.00333.
A Bonferroni correction, not a tuned knob. It needs 199–999 controls depending on
family, against measured control populations of 27k–366k.

## Open decisions that are the maintainer's, not the implementer's

1. **The censoring rule.** A REDSEA-clamped `0.0` is currently emitted as
   `CELL_NEGATIVE` (`restore_stage.py:386`) — a left-censored cell reported as a
   confident biological negative. Whether that is wrong is a real question:
   below-zero means either the signal was entirely spillover (absence →
   `negative` is right) or the correction over-subtracted (→ `unavailable`).
   **Measure before changing**: the `raw__` distribution and contact degree of
   censored vs uncensored cells, and the size of the `Other → Unavailable`
   conversion. Predominantly low-raw/low-contact → keep `negative` and record an
   audit flag; high-raw/high-contact → `unavailable` with reason. This matters:
   a cell stays `Other` only when **all 22** gate markers are explicitly
   negative, so the conservative choice could convert much of the 31–35% `Other`
   population into `Unavailable`.
2. **`broad_immune` still lists CD11b** in the alpha family (10 members) though
   the gate has 9. The nine real markers are corrected as if there were ten —
   conservative, but it is drift. Pinned by a `DRIFT`-prefixed test.
3. **Vimentin** is positive in 55% of all `Ambiguous` cells and 94% of
