# Resolving tied endocrine protein identities

The pancreas subtype producer first runs its existing image QC, hormone-fragment exclusion, islet/single admission and evidence checks. A positive exact tie among the leading measured hormone signals is not a named subtype yet. The producer compares **only those tied contenders** in the cell's original level-zero QuPath segmentation and qptiff.

Rasterize the original polygons, including holes and later ownership overwrites. Remove a two-pixel rim (5 × 5 square erosion). Apply a 3 × 3 median to each native channel and transform its values using that donor's alignment multiplier and the corresponding marker's cohort floor and span, without clipping. Each interior pixel votes once for the unique highest eligible contender, using the original donor gate; an equal maximum or all-below-gate pixel abstains. All interior pixels, including abstentions, stay in the denominator. The leading contender must have at least 15 percentage points more area than the runner-up **and** a strictly higher median intensity than every contender on the same interior. UMAP coordinates and neighbours do not enter this call.

Read the decision and its counts, medians and fractional area lead from `endocrine_subtype_status` and `endocrine_interior_evidence`; multiply `area_lead` by 100 for percentage points. If the requirements are not met or image evidence is unavailable, ordinary cells retain the ambiguous subtype and unresolved direct Singles retain their exclusion. The primary label remains distinct from the downstream refinement. Previously written tables and plots do not update automatically.

For portable runs, bind the image/segmentation with `IO60_QUPATH_MANIFEST` and the exact upstream alignment's `scales.csv` with `IO60_PIXEL_ALIGNMENT_SCALES` (the Stage 06 runner does this). The historical development-alignment route can use its matching `IO60_SHIFTS_PATH` instead. The typing scale supplies the per-marker floor and span. The resolver does not infer missing calibration from other donors.
